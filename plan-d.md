# PLAN D: Source Document

**Title:** Summary of Benefits and Coverage: What this Plan Covers & What You Pay for Covered Services
**Plan:** Northwestern University: Choice Plus PPO Plan (UnitedHealthcare) | Coverage for: Family | Plan Type: PPO
**Coverage Period:** 01/01/2026 – 12/31/2026
**Source:** Northwestern University Human Resources
(https://hr.northwestern.edu/documents/benefits/sbc-ppo-2026.pdf)
This is the official SBC for Northwestern University's PPO medical plan, posted publicly on the
university's HR site for employees and prospective employees.

## Important Questions

- **What is the overall deductible?** Network: $750 individual / $1,500 family. Out-of-network: $1,500
  individual / $3,000 family. Per calendar year.
- **Are there services covered before you meet your deductible?** Yes. Preventive care services and
  categories with a copay are covered before the deductible is met.
- **Are there other deductibles for specific services?** No.
- **What is the out-of-pocket limit for this plan?** Network: $3,000 individual / $6,000 family.
  Out-of-network: $6,000 individual / $12,000 family. Separate Rx out-of-pocket max: $1,500 individual
  / $3,000 family.
- **What is not included in the out-of-pocket limit?** Premiums, balance-billing charges, health care
  this plan doesn't cover, and penalties for failure to obtain preauthorization for services.
- **Will you pay less if you use a network provider?** Yes.
- **Do you need a referral to see a specialist?** No. You can see the specialist you choose without a
  referral.

## Common Medical Events: What You Will Pay (Network / Out-of-Network)

All copayment and coinsurance costs shown are after your deductible has been met, if a deductible applies.

- Primary care visit to treat an injury or illness: $40 copay/visit, deductible does not apply / 40%
  coinsurance
- Specialist visit: $55 copay/visit, deductible does not apply / 40% coinsurance
- Preventive care/screening/immunization: No charge / 40% coinsurance
- Diagnostic test (x-ray, blood work): 20% coinsurance / 40% coinsurance
- Imaging (CT/PET scans, MRIs): 20% coinsurance / 40% coinsurance
- Tier 1 drugs (lowest cost): $10 copay retail / $20 mail-order, deductible does not apply (same both
  network and out-of-network)
- Tier 2 drugs: $50 copay retail / $100 mail-order, deductible does not apply
- Tier 3 drugs: $75 copay retail / $150 mail-order, deductible does not apply
- Tier 4 drugs (highest cost): $100 copay retail / $200 mail-order, deductible does not apply
- Outpatient surgery facility fee: 20% coinsurance / (preauthorization required out-of-network)
- Outpatient surgery physician/surgeon fees: 20% coinsurance / 40% coinsurance
- Emergency room care: $150 copay/visit then 20% coinsurance, deductible does not apply / same as
  network
- Emergency medical transportation: 20% coinsurance (network deductible applies) / 20% coinsurance
- Urgent care: 20% coinsurance / 40% coinsurance
- Hospital facility fee: 20% coinsurance / 40% coinsurance
- Hospital physician/surgeon fees: 20% coinsurance / 40% coinsurance
- Mental health/behavioral health/substance abuse outpatient services: $40 copay/visit, deductible
  does not apply / 40% coinsurance
- Mental health/behavioral health/substance abuse inpatient services: 20% coinsurance / 40% coinsurance
- Pregnancy office visits: No charge / 40% coinsurance
- Childbirth/delivery professional services: 20% coinsurance / 40% coinsurance
- Childbirth/delivery facility services: 20% coinsurance / 40% coinsurance
- Home health care: 20% coinsurance / 40% coinsurance
- Rehabilitation services: 20% coinsurance / 40% coinsurance. Unlimited visits per calendar year.
- Habilitation services: 20% coinsurance / 40% coinsurance
- Skilled nursing care: 20% coinsurance / 40% coinsurance
- Durable medical equipment: 20% coinsurance / 40% coinsurance
- Hospice services: 20% coinsurance / 40% coinsurance
- Children's eye exam: Not covered / Not covered
- Children's glasses: Not covered / Not covered
- Children's dental check-up: Not covered / Not covered

## Excluded Services (Plan Generally Does NOT Cover)
Acupuncture, Cosmetic surgery, Dental care, Glasses, Long-term care, Prescription drugs (covered
under separate Rx benefit, not this medical benefit), Routine eye care, Routine foot care (except as
covered for diabetes), Weight loss programs.

## Other Covered Services (limitations may apply)
Bariatric surgery, Chiropractic (manipulative) care, Hearing aids, Infertility treatment,
Non-emergency care when traveling outside the U.S., Private-duty nursing (outpatient only).

## Does this plan provide Minimum Essential Coverage? Yes.
## Does this plan meet Minimum Value Standards? Yes.

## Coverage Examples

**Peg is Having a Baby** (9 months of in-network pre-natal care and a hospital delivery)
Total Example Cost: $12,700. Peg's cost sharing: Deductibles $750, Copayments $0, Coinsurance $2,100,
Limits/exclusions $60. **The total Peg would pay is $2,910.**

**Managing Joe's Type 2 Diabetes** (a year of routine in-network care of a well-controlled condition)
Total Example Cost: $5,600. Joe's cost sharing: Deductibles $300, Copayments $300, Coinsurance $0,
Limits/exclusions $0. **The total Joe would pay is $600.**

**Mia's Simple Fracture** (in-network emergency room visit and follow-up care)
Total Example Cost: $2,800. Mia's cost sharing: Deductibles $750, Copayments $200, Coinsurance $400,
Limits/exclusions $0. **The total Mia would pay is $1,350.**
