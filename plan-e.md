# PLAN E — Source Document

**Title:** Summary of Benefits and Coverage: What this Plan Covers & What it Costs
**Plan:** Cochise Combined Trust — EPO Plan (BlueCross BlueShield of Arizona network) | Coverage for: Single + Family | Plan Type: EPO
**Coverage Period:** 07/01/2025 – 06/30/2026
**Source:** Cochise County, Arizona government
(https://www.cochise.az.gov/DocumentCenter/View/21707/EPO-Summary-of-Benefits-Fiscal-Year-2025-to-2026-PDF)
This is the official SBC for the EPO plan offered to Cochise Combined Trust members, a local
government employee benefit trust, published on the county's public website.

## Important Questions

- **What is the overall deductible?** $600 per participant / $1,800 per family.
- **Are there services covered before you meet your deductible?** Yes. Preventive care, office visit
  copayments, ambulance, and prescription drug copayments are covered before the deductible is met.
- **Are there other deductibles for specific services?** No.
- **What is the out-of-pocket limit for this plan?** $9,100 per participant / $18,200 per family.
- **What is not included in the out-of-pocket limit?** Premiums, balance-billed charges, and health
  care this plan doesn't cover.
- **Will you pay less if you use a network provider?** This plan only covers in-network care — there is
  no coverage for out-of-network providers except in a medical emergency.
- **Do you need a referral to see a specialist?** No. You can see the specialist you choose without a
  referral.

## Common Medical Events — What You Will Pay (In-Network / Out-of-Network)

All copayment and coinsurance costs shown are after your deductible has been met, if a deductible applies.

- Primary care visit to treat an injury or illness: $30 copay/visit / Not covered
- Specialist visit: $45 copay/visit / Not covered
- Preventive care/screening/immunization: No charge / Not covered
- Diagnostic test (x-ray, blood work): $30–$45 copay for charges under $500 depending on provider
  type; 20% coinsurance after deductible for a single test over $500 / Not covered
- Imaging (CT/PET scans, MRIs): 20% coinsurance after deductible / Not covered. Preauthorization
  required.
- Generic drugs: $10 copay/30-day supply, $20 copay/90-day supply / Not covered
- Preferred brand drugs: $30 copay/30-day supply, $60 copay/90-day supply / Not covered
- Non-preferred brand drugs: $60 copay/30-day supply, $120 copay/90-day supply / Not covered
- Specialty drugs: 20% copay up to $150 for a 30-day supply / Not covered
- Outpatient surgery facility fee: 20% coinsurance after deductible / Not covered. Preauthorization
  required for procedures over $1,000.
- Outpatient surgery physician/surgeon fees: 20% coinsurance after deductible for office surgery over
  $500; copay for office surgery under $500 / Not covered
- Emergency room care: $250 copay/occurrence plus 20% coinsurance after deductible (copay waived if
  admitted); non-emergency ER use not covered
- Emergency medical transportation: 20% coinsurance, deductible does not apply. Non-emergency
  transport not covered.
- Urgent care: $45 copay/occurrence / Not covered
- Hospital facility fee: 20% coinsurance after deductible / Not covered. Preauthorization required.
- Hospital physician/surgeon fees: 20% coinsurance after deductible / Not covered
- Mental health/behavioral health/substance abuse outpatient services: $30 copay (PCP) or $45 copay
  (specialist) per visit / Not covered
- Mental health/behavioral health/substance abuse inpatient services: 20% coinsurance after
  deductible / Not covered. Preauthorization required.
- Pregnancy office visits: $30 copay for initial visit only / Not covered
- Childbirth/delivery professional services: 20% coinsurance after deductible / Not covered
- Childbirth/delivery facility services: 20% coinsurance after deductible / Not covered
- Home health care: 20% coinsurance after deductible / Not covered. Plan year maximum 60 visits.
- Rehabilitation services: $10 copay/visit for non-hospital-based PT/OT; 20% coinsurance after
  deductible for all other rehab / Not covered. Speech therapy limited to 20 visits/plan year;
  inpatient therapy limited to 60 days/plan year.
- Habilitation services: Not covered / Not covered
- Skilled nursing care: 20% coinsurance after deductible / Not covered. Limited to 90 days/plan year.
- Durable medical equipment: 20% coinsurance after deductible / Not covered. Preauthorization required
  for items over $1,000.
- Hospice services: 20% coinsurance after deductible / Not covered. Limited to 60 days per 12
  consecutive months.
- Children's eye exam: No charge / Not covered. Limited to one routine eye exam per plan participant.
- Children's glasses: Not covered / Not covered (covered under a separate stand-alone vision plan)
- Children's dental check-up: Not covered / Not covered (covered under a separate stand-alone dental
  plan)

## Excluded Services (Plan Generally Does NOT Cover)
Acupuncture, Ambulance transportation for a non-medical emergency, Cosmetic surgery (except
reconstructive surgery and correction of congenital defects), Dental care, Emergency room services
for a non-medical emergency, Glasses, Habilitation services, Infertility treatment (except
diagnosis), Long-term care, Non-emergency out-of-network care, Non-emergency care when traveling
outside the U.S., Prescription drugs from a non-network pharmacy, Private-duty nursing, Routine eye
care (except one routine eye exam), Routine foot care (except as medically necessary), Weight loss
programs.

## Other Covered Services (limitations may apply)
Bariatric surgery (for treatment of morbid obesity only), Chiropractic care (limited to 20
visits/plan year), Hearing aids (limited to 2 aids every 3 years, $2,000 maximum benefit).

## Does this plan provide Minimum Essential Coverage? Yes.
## Does this plan meet the Minimum Value Standards? Yes.

## Coverage Examples

**Peg is Having a Baby** (9 months of in-network pre-natal care and a hospital delivery)
Total Example Cost: $12,700. Peg's cost sharing: Deductibles $600, Copayments $45, Coinsurance
$2,400, Limits/exclusions $20. **The total Peg would pay is $3,065.**

**Managing Joe's Type 2 Diabetes** (a year of routine in-network care of a well-controlled condition)
Total Example Cost: $5,600. Joe's cost sharing: Deductibles $600, Copayments $550, Coinsurance $60,
Limits/exclusions $200. **The total Joe would pay is $1,410.**

**Mia's Simple Fracture** (in-network emergency room visit and follow-up care)
Total Example Cost: $2,800. Mia's cost sharing: Deductibles $600, Copayments $450, Coinsurance $300,
Limits/exclusions $0. **The total Mia would pay is $1,350.**
