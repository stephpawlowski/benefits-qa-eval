Benefits Q&A Checker

Will an AI stick strictly to what a benefits document actually says, instead of guessing at coverage details?
65 questions answered strictly from two real Summary of Benefits and Coverage (SBC) documents (an official CMS reference PPO plan, and Auburn University's actual 2025 HDHP plan), scored against an answer key verified against the source PDFs — including 15 adversarial cases designed to test whether the model says "Not stated in the document" instead of guessing, on facts that are genuinely absent, on invented plan names, and on questions that borrow one plan's specifics to bait it into misapplying them to the other. Model tested: claude-sonnet-5. Full write-up on GitHub.

Try it with your own document

Paste in any Summary of Benefits and Coverage (or similar plan document) and ask it a question. This sends your document and question to claude-sonnet-5 through a rate-limited proxy — no API key needed on your end, and nothing is stored. The model is instructed to answer only from what you paste in, and to say "Not stated in the document" rather than guess.
