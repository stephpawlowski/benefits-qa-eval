# PLAN F — Source Document

**Title:** Summary of Benefits and Coverage: What this Plan Covers & What You Pay For Covered Services
**Plan:** CalPERS Access+ HMO (Blue Shield of California) | Coverage for: Individual + Family | Plan Type: HMO
**Coverage Period:** 1/1/2025 – 12/31/2025
**Source:** Blue Shield of California, for the California Public Employees' Retirement System (CalPERS)
(https://www.blueshieldca.com/content/dam/bsca/en/premier-accounts/docs/calpers/Public_Employees_Benefit_Retirement_System_CalPERS_Access_HMO__M0037791_01-25_SBC.pdf)
This is the official SBC for the Access+ HMO plan offered to California state and public agency
employees through CalPERS, published on Blue Shield of California's public site.

## Important Questions

- **What is the overall deductible?** $0.
- **Are there services covered before you meet your deductible?** Yes. Preventive care and services
  listed in the complete terms of coverage.
- **Are there other deductibles for specific services?** No.
- **What is the out-of-pocket limit for this plan?** Medical: $1,500 individual / $3,000 family.
  Pharmacy: $7,700 individual / $15,400 family (includes $1,000 for mail-service formulary
  prescriptions per member).
- **What is not included in the out-of-pocket limit?** Copayments for certain services, premiums, and
  health care this plan doesn't cover.
- **Will you pay less if you use a network provider?** Yes.
- **Do you need a referral to see a specialist?** Yes. This plan pays some or all of the cost to see a
  specialist only if you have a referral first (self-referral is available for Access+ Specialist
  visits specifically).

## Common Medical Events — What You Will Pay (Plan Provider / Non-Plan Provider)

All copayment and coinsurance costs shown are after your deductible has been met, if a deductible applies.

- Primary care visit to treat an injury or illness: $15/visit / Not covered
- Specialist visit: Access+ Specialist $30/visit, Other Specialist $15/visit / Not covered
- Preventive care/screening/immunization: No charge / Not covered
- Diagnostic test (x-ray, blood work): No charge / Not covered. Preauthorization required.
- Imaging (CT/PET scans, MRIs): No charge / Not covered. Preauthorization required.
- Tier 1 drugs (lowest cost): $5/prescription retail, $10/prescription mail order / Not covered
- Tier 2 drugs: $20/prescription retail, $40/prescription mail order / Not covered
- Tier 3 drugs: $50/prescription retail, $100/prescription mail order / Not covered
- Tier 4 drugs (specialty): $30/prescription retail, $60/prescription mail order, limited to
  Network Specialty Pharmacies / Not covered
- Outpatient surgery facility fee: No charge / Not covered
- Outpatient surgery physician/surgeon fees: No charge / Not covered
- Emergency room care: $50/visit facility fee, no charge physician fee / same as in-network (applies
  regardless of network since this is emergency care)
- Emergency medical transportation: No charge / No charge
- Urgent care: $15/visit / Not covered within the plan service area; $15/visit outside the plan
  service area
- Hospital facility fee: No charge / Not covered. Preauthorization required.
- Hospital physician/surgeon fees: No charge / Not covered
- Mental health/behavioral health/substance abuse outpatient services: $15/visit office visit, no
  charge for other outpatient services / Not covered
- Mental health/behavioral health/substance abuse inpatient services: No charge / Not covered.
  Preauthorization required.
- Pregnancy office visits: No charge / Not covered
- Childbirth/delivery professional services: No charge / Not covered
- Childbirth/delivery facility services: No charge / Not covered
- Home health care: No charge / Not covered. Preauthorization required.
- Rehabilitation services: $15/visit / Not covered
- Habilitation services: $15/visit / Not covered
- Skilled nursing care: No charge / Not covered. Limited to 100 days per member per benefit period.
- Durable medical equipment: No charge / Not covered. Preauthorization required.
- Hospice services: No charge / Not covered. Preauthorization required except for pre-hospice
  consultation.
- Children's eye exam: No charge / Not covered
- Children's glasses: Not covered / Not covered
- Children's dental check-up: Not covered / Not covered

## Excluded Services (Plan Generally Does NOT Cover)
Cosmetic surgery, Dental care (Adult), Long-term care, Non-emergency care when traveling outside the
U.S., Private-duty nursing, Routine eye care (Adult), Routine foot care, Weight loss programs.

## Other Covered Services (limitations may apply)
Acupuncture, Bariatric surgery, Chiropractic care, Hearing aids, Infertility treatment.

## Does this plan provide Minimum Essential Coverage? Yes.
## Does this plan meet the Minimum Value Standards? Yes.

**Mia's Simple Fracture** (participating emergency room visit and follow-up care)
